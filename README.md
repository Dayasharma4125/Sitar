# 🎵 Sitar

**A fully offline, beautifully expressive local music player for Android.**

Sitar plays the music that's already on your phone, and nothing else. No accounts, no ads, no streaming, no internet. It's built with **Kotlin**, **Jetpack Compose** and **Material 3 Expressive**, and it handles nearly every audio format you can throw at it, from everyday AAC and MP3 to lossless FLAC and Hi-Res files.

I built this with a mix of Google AI Studio and my own hands-on coding, because I wanted a player that feels as good as it sounds.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎧 Playback
- Plays almost all local audio formats (AAC, MP3, FLAC, Hi-Res and more)
- Full now-playing screen with seek bar, volume control, shuffle, repeat and favorite
- Playing queue, sleep timer and cast support
- Output device chip so you always know where the sound is going
- Live audio visualizer (waveform) on the player screen

### 🏠 Library
- **All Songs**, **Favorites**, **Most Played** and **Hi-Res** views
- Sort and reverse-sort your library in one tap
- One-tap **Shuffle All**
- Artists grid with album art and track/album counts
- Fast search across songs, artists and albums, including lossless albums

### 📊 Listening Analytics
- Built-in **Music Tracker** with total plays, total listen time, top artist and favorite song
- Ranked "Most Listened" list with play counts, listen duration and last-played time
- **Play Top List** and **Shuffle Top** to jump straight into your favorites

### 🎤 Lyrics
- Full-screen synced lyrics with karaoke-style word highlighting
- Instrumental break indicators
- Works with any language, including Cyrillic and Devanagari scripts

### 🎨 Customization
- **Material 3 Expressive Studio**: tweak shapes, motion physics, audio visualizers and screen modules
- **Lyrics & Typography Studio**: text size, alignment, contrast, backdrop styles and karaoke animations
- **Home Screen Atmosphere & Effects**: visual mood and effects for the home screen
- Dynamic theming that adapts to the album art

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone the repository
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/<your-username>/<repo-name>.git
   ```
2. Open the project in **Android Studio**
3. Let Gradle sync, then run it on a device or emulator
4. Grant storage / audio permission when asked, and Sitar will scan your music

---

## 🔒 Privacy

Everything stays on your device. Sitar has no ads, no trackers, no login and no network dependency. Your listening stats are stored locally and can be cleared any time.
